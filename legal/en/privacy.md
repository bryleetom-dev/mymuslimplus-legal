# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026

Your privacy is important to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how **MyMuslimPlus** handles information when you use the application.

## 1. Information We Collect

MyMuslimPlus is designed with privacy in mind.

The application does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Application settings and preferences are primarily stored locally on your device.

## 2. Location Information

If you enable location access, MyMuslimPlus may use your device's location to determine your approximate geographic location and provide location-based features, including prayer times.

Location information may be used to determine your city, region, country, timezone, latitude, and longitude as necessary to provide prayer-related functionality.

Where possible, location information and related preferences are stored locally on your device.

MyMuslimPlus does not continuously track or monitor your location.

Location access is optional and can be disabled at any time through your device settings. Disabling location access may prevent certain location-based features from functioning correctly.

If you travel or move to another location, you may need to refresh your location so that prayer times and related information can be updated.

## 3. Prayer Time Data

MyMuslimPlus may connect to external prayer-time services or public data sources to obtain prayer schedules.

Depending on the feature and data source being used, information such as your selected location, coordinates, date, or calculation method may be used to obtain the relevant prayer schedule.

Prayer schedules may also be cached or stored locally on your device to improve performance and reduce unnecessary network requests.

## 4. Notifications

If you enable prayer notifications, MyMuslimPlus schedules notifications or alarms on your device for prayer reminders and Adhan notifications.

Notification preferences are stored locally on your device.

You can change or disable notification permissions and preferences at any time through the application or your device settings.

## 5. Local Storage

MyMuslimPlus stores certain application data locally on your device, which may include:

* Preferred language
* Theme preference
* Prayer calculation preferences
* Prayer notification settings
* Saved location
* Quran preferences
* Reading preferences
* Other application settings

This locally stored information is not intentionally transmitted to MyMuslimPlus servers.

## 6. Quran and Islamic Content

MyMuslimPlus may provide Quran text, translations, transliterations, Tajweed information, and other Islamic content.

Some content may be bundled with the application, while other content may be obtained from external data sources.

The application may store downloaded or retrieved content locally on your device to improve performance and support offline functionality where available.

## 7. Third-Party Services

MyMuslimPlus may use third-party services, APIs, public data sources, hosting providers, or other infrastructure to provide certain features.

These services may process limited technical or feature-related information necessary to fulfill a request.

MyMuslimPlus does not intentionally use advertising networks, behavioral tracking technologies, or analytics services to create advertising profiles about users.

MyMuslimPlus does not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms, which may differ from this Privacy Policy.

## 8. Donations

MyMuslimPlus may provide an optional donation feature to support continued development.

If you choose to make a donation, the transaction may be processed by a third-party payment provider.

MyMuslimPlus does not intentionally collect or store your payment card number, bank account credentials, or other payment credentials.

Any payment information submitted during a donation is handled according to the applicable payment provider's privacy policy and terms.

## 9. Camera and Other Device Permissions

Certain features of MyMuslimPlus may require additional device permissions.

For example, camera access may be required for features that use the device camera.

Permissions are only requested when needed for the relevant feature. You can deny or revoke permissions through your device settings, although doing so may prevent certain features from working.

## 10. Children's Privacy

MyMuslimPlus is intended for users of all ages.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because MyMuslimPlus does not require an account and is designed to minimize personal data collection, users can generally use the application without providing personal identifying information.

## 11. Data Security

MyMuslimPlus is designed to minimize the collection of personal information and keep application data on your device whenever reasonably possible.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the application.

However, no method of electronic storage, processing, or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 12. Data Retention and Deletion

Because MyMuslimPlus does not require an account, there is generally no user profile or account data maintained by MyMuslimPlus that requires deletion.

Locally stored application data can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application from your device.

Information processed by third-party services may be subject to those providers' own retention and deletion policies.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to MyMuslimPlus, its features, or applicable requirements.

If significant changes are made, the updated version and its effective date will be published within the application or through an appropriate distribution channel.

Your continued use of MyMuslimPlus after the revised Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
