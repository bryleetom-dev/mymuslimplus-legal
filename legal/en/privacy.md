# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 16, 2026**

Your privacy is important to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how **MyMuslim+** handles information when you use the application.

## 1. Information We Collect

MyMuslim+ is designed with privacy in mind.

The application does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Application settings and preferences are primarily stored locally on your device.

MyMuslim+ does not intentionally use advertising networks, behavioral tracking technologies, or analytics services to build profiles about users.

## 2. Location Information

If you enable location access, MyMuslim+ may use your device's location to determine your geographic location and provide location-based features, including prayer times.

Location information may include your latitude, longitude, city, region, country, or timezone when necessary to provide prayer-related functionality.

When required to obtain prayer times from an external service, relevant location information may be transmitted to that service as part of the request.

Where reasonably possible, location information and related preferences are stored locally on your device.

MyMuslim+ does not continuously track or monitor your location.

Location access is optional and can be disabled at any time through your device settings. Disabling location access may prevent certain location-based features from functioning correctly.

If you travel or move to another location, you may need to refresh your location so that prayer times and related information can be updated.

## 3. Prayer Time Data

MyMuslim+ may connect to external prayer-time services or public data sources to obtain prayer schedules.

Depending on the feature and data source being used, information such as your selected location, coordinates, date, timezone, or prayer calculation method may be used to obtain the relevant prayer schedule.

MyMuslim+ may also apply your selected Ihtiyati adjustment to prayer times according to your settings.

Prayer schedules and related data may be cached or stored locally on your device to improve performance and reduce unnecessary network requests.

## 4. Local Storage

MyMuslim+ stores certain application data locally on your device, which may include:

- Preferred language
- Theme preference
- Prayer calculation method
- Ihtiyati adjustment
- Saved location or location preferences
- Quran preferences
- Reading preferences
- Other application settings

MyMuslim+ does not require an account to store these preferences. Locally stored information is not intentionally transmitted to MyMuslim+ servers.

## 5. Quran and Islamic Content

MyMuslim+ may provide Quran text, translations, transliterations, Tajweed information, and other Islamic content.

Some content may be bundled with the application, while other content may be obtained from external data sources.

The application may store downloaded or retrieved content locally on your device to improve performance and support offline functionality where available.

## 6. Third-Party Services

MyMuslim+ may use third-party services, APIs, public data sources, hosting providers, or other infrastructure to provide certain features.

These services may process limited technical or feature-related information necessary to fulfill a request.

For example, prayer-time services may receive information such as a location, date, timezone, or selected calculation method when required to provide prayer schedules.

MyMuslim+ does not intentionally use advertising networks, behavioral tracking technologies, or analytics services to create advertising profiles about users.

MyMuslim+ does not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms, which may differ from this Privacy Policy.

## 7. Donations

MyMuslim+ may provide an optional donation feature to support continued development.

If you choose to make a donation, the transaction may be processed by a third-party payment provider.

MyMuslim+ does not intentionally collect or store your payment card number, bank account credentials, or other payment credentials.

Any payment information submitted during a donation is handled according to the applicable payment provider's privacy policy and terms.

## 8. Camera and Other Device Permissions

Certain features of MyMuslim+ may require additional device permissions.

Camera access is used only by features that require the device camera and only when the relevant feature is being used and permission has been granted.

Other device permissions may be requested when necessary to provide specific application functionality.

You can deny or revoke permissions through your device settings, although doing so may prevent certain features from working correctly.

## 9. Internet and Network Communication

MyMuslim+ requires Internet access for certain features that depend on external services or online data sources.

Network communication may be used to retrieve or update information required by those features.

The application does not intentionally use Internet access to collect personal information for advertising or behavioral tracking purposes.

Information sent to third-party services is limited to what is reasonably necessary for the requested feature, subject to the privacy practices of those services.

## 10. Children's Privacy

MyMuslim+ is intended for users of all ages.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because MyMuslim+ does not require an account and is designed to minimize personal data collection, users can generally use the application without providing personal identifying information.

## 11. Data Security

MyMuslim+ is designed to minimize the collection of personal information and keep application data on your device whenever reasonably possible.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage, processing, or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 12. Data Retention and Deletion

Because MyMuslim+ does not require an account, there is generally no user profile or account data maintained by MyMuslim+ that requires deletion.

Locally stored application data can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application from your device.

Information processed by third-party services may be subject to those providers' own retention and deletion policies.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to MyMuslim+, its features, services, or applicable requirements.

If significant changes are made, the updated version and its effective date will be published within the application or through an appropriate distribution channel.
